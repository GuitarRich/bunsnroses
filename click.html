Tap Click and the whole screen becomes the lamp — a dim red flash on every beat, a bright one on the 1, with a click through the speaker you can mute. Prop the phone on the kit and count yourself in. The tempo is the band's, not the record's: hit Tap four times in time and it takes the tempo from your hands, or nudge it with − and +. Save writes it to the Tempos tab of the sheet, so everyone else's click runs at the same speed — you can type into that tab directly instead if you'd rather. Anything not saved there falls back to the song's catalog BPM.
